# App Store Connect: Add Reviewer Note for Email Verification (Click-by-Click)

So the App Review tester knows how to complete sign-up and email verification, add the note below in App Store Connect.

---

## Where to paste the note

1. Go to **https://appstoreconnect.apple.com** and sign in.
2. Click **My Apps**.
3. Click your **Scroll Mine / ValueMiner** app.
4. In the left sidebar, under **App Store**, click **App Information** (or select the version you’re submitting, then look for **App Review Information** / **Review Information**).
5. Find the **“Notes”** or **“Review Notes”** or **“App Review Information”** section (often under “Version Information” or on the same page as the build).
6. If there’s already text in **Notes**, add the verification note at the top or bottom. If it’s empty, paste the full note from **APP_STORE_REVIEWER_NOTE_VERIFICATION.txt**.
7. Click **Save**.

---

## Exact place (if your layout differs)

- Sometimes the notes are under: **App Store** → **[Your version]** → **App Review Information** → **Notes**.
- Or: **App Store** → **App Information** → scroll to **App Review Information** → **Notes**.

Paste the contents of **APP_STORE_REVIEWER_NOTE_VERIFICATION.txt** (without the “START COPY” / “END COPY” lines) into that **Notes** field and save.
